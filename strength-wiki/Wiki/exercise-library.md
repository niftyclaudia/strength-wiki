# Exercise Library

Setup and cues for each exercise in the program, grouped by movement pattern. Equipment: squat rack, barbell + plates, kettlebell, bands.

---

## Squat

**Barbell back squat.** Bar on upper traps, safety pins just below bottom depth. Brace (breathe into belly, tighten), sit down between the heels, knees track over toes, drive up through the whole foot.

**Kettlebell reverse lunge (goblet).** Hold the KB at the chest. Step back, lower the back knee toward the floor, most of the weight on the front heel. Push through the front foot to return.

## Hinge

**Barbell Romanian deadlift.** Start standing with the bar (take it from the rack at mid-thigh). Soft knees, push hips back, bar slides down the thighs. Stop when hamstrings are stretched and the back is still flat, usually just below the knee.

**Kettlebell single-leg RDL.** KB in the opposite hand to the standing leg. Hinge forward, back leg extends behind in a straight line with the torso. Hips stay square to the floor. Balance matters more than load.

**Barbell glute bridge.** Lie on the floor, bar over hip crease (use a pad or towel). Feet flat, shins vertical at the top. Drive hips up, squeeze glutes, ribs down, pause 1s.

## Push

**Barbell overhead press.** Bar from the rack at collarbone height. Squeeze glutes, ribs down, press straight up and move your head "through the window" at the top.

**Barbell floor press.** Set the safety pins low in the rack and lie on the floor under them. Unrack, lower until the upper arms touch the floor, pause, press. Use it instead of a bench press when there's no bench.

**Push-up.** Hands under shoulders, body in one line, elbows about 45° from the torso. If 8 reps aren't possible, elevate your hands on the rack's barbell.

**Half-kneeling KB overhead press.** Kneel on the side of the pressing arm. Squeeze the down-knee glute, press the KB straight up without leaning.

## Pull

**Barbell bent-over row.** Hinge to about 45°, flat back, pull the bar to the lower ribs, lower under control.

**Kettlebell single-arm row.** One hand on the rack or your knee in a split stance. Pull the elbow toward your back pocket.

**Band lat pulldown.** Anchor the band high on the rack. Kneel, pull your elbows down to your ribs, squeeze the lats.

**Band pull-apart.** Arms straight at shoulder height, pull the band to your chest by squeezing the shoulder blades.

## Core / Carry

**Dead bug.** Lie on your back, arms up, knees at 90°. Low back pressed to the floor. Extend opposite arm and leg slowly, then return.

**Side plank.** Elbow under shoulder, hips high, body in one line.

**Band Pallof press.** Band anchored at chest height to the side. Press it straight out and resist the rotation. Hold 2s.

**KB suitcase carry.** KB in one hand, walk tall without leaning toward the weight.

## Calves

**Single-leg calf raise.** Stand on a plate or step edge, hold the rack for balance and a KB in the other hand. Full range: deep stretch at the bottom, pause at the top.
