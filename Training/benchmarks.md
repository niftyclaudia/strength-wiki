Except for the measured two-mile row, these are Riegel formula equivalents using
`T2 = T1 × (D2 / D1)^1.06`, with the unrounded test duration of 1118.719 seconds and
consistent distance units. Paces and splits are rounded independently to the nearest
second; calculate longer repetitions from the unrounded pace, not multiples of the
rounded 400 m split. No heat adjustment is included.

The equivalents assume endurance scales with distance; they are not individually tested
paces or validated race targets. The marathon reference is especially provisional.
Research in recreational runners found better calibration through the half marathon and
overly optimistic marathon estimates: [Vickers & Vertosick, 2016](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5000509/).
